## Project Overview
This project features an interactive **Sales & Product Performance Dashboard** designed to evaluate revenue distribution across product categories, sales channels, and geographical regions.

The main objective is to provide actionable insights into product group performance, regional sales volume, and category-level demand.

---

## Key Metrics & Insights
* **Category Performance:** Analyzed revenue generated by key product groups such as Beverages, Energy Drinks, Tea, Iced Tea, Juices, and Water.
* **Regional Distribution:** Evaluated sales volume across primary regional markets compared to Baku.
* **Product Type Breakdown:** Measured revenue contribution by specific product lines and varieties.
* **Filter Dynamics:** Integrated dynamic slicers for Year, Month, Day, Category, and Region to allow custom deep-dive analysis.

---

## Tools Used
* **Visualization & Analytics:** Power BI / Excel
* **Data Visuals:** Slicers, KPI Cards, Bar Charts, Donut Charts, Data Tables
